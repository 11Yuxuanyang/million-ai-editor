# Script To Cover

Use this reference to turn a稿子, outline, transcript, or topic paragraph into thumbnail-safe cover text.

## Extraction Order

1. Identify the **external promise**: what the audience can understand without knowing the creator.
2. Identify the **stakes**: money, time, status, challenge, risk, transformation, conflict, or proof.
3. Identify the **format**: challenge, experiment, product launch, teardown, confession, tutorial, documentary, review, or announcement.
4. Identify the **visible proof**: income number, product screenshot, task list, people, before/after, deadline, ranking, receipt, roadmap, or failure signal.
5. Identify the **one thing to show**, not all things mentioned in the script.

## Title Selection

Use short, concrete, public-facing text. Prefer:

- `全民AI百万挑战`
- `0资源开科技公司`
- `一年赚100万`
- `AI创业实录`
- `公开收入`
- `第2期`

Avoid:

- Principles that require context: "不做中转站生意"
- Long mission statements
- Legal/tax/public policy claims as the main title
- Full episode summaries
- More than one big idea in the main title

## Cover Text Shape

Default structure:

```text
Badge: 第N期
Main title: 6-12 Chinese characters, or one compact mixed Chinese/English phrase
Subtitle: one concrete promise or angle
Task/status block: optional 2-4 short rows
```

For challenge/event videos, use a task card:

```text
挑战任务
01 公开收入
02 做出产品
03 赚到100万
```

For product videos, use a product card:

```text
产品预告
01 恋爱APP
02 电商Agent
03 Agent社交
```

For revenue videos, use a proof card:

```text
本期进度
收入 ¥____
成本 ¥____
剩余 ____天
```

## Hook Ranking

Choose the hook with the highest combination of:

- **Specificity**: has a number, deadline, audience, or concrete artifact.
- **Tension**: contains a hard constraint or visible risk.
- **Participation**: makes viewers feel they can join, copy, or judge.
- **Proof potential**: can be backed by screenshots, tasks, or public updates.

If two hooks compete, pick the one a stranger can understand in under one second.

## Compression Examples

Script says: "在资源、资金匮乏，人力人才短缺的条件下，赚到人生第一个一百万，开一家科技公司并拥有稳定现金流。"

Cover text:

```text
全民AI百万挑战
0资源开科技公司
```

Script says: "我们会公开收入，保证可以借鉴、抄袭，不逃税漏税。"

Cover text:

```text
公开收入
大家监督
```

Script says: "产品预告：恋爱APP、电商Agent、Agent社交产品。"

Cover text:

```text
3个AI产品
开始变现
```

## Risk Handling

- Do not over-promise guaranteed income unless the script explicitly frames it as a challenge or experiment.
- Do not imply government endorsement, investor endorsement, or partnership unless confirmed.
- Keep money claims framed as goals or documented progress when not yet achieved.
